I build AI systems that people can rely on.

I'm an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at Nanyang Technological University and a graduate researcher in Prof. Erik Cambria's SenticNet Lab. I build AI systems end to end and make sure they hold up on real data: a live web and mobile platform with an LLM assistant, LLM evaluation and multi-agent pipelines, multimodal deep learning for city-scale digital twins at A*STAR, and efficient fine-tuning and inference.

Before my master's, I spent 2 years at NielsenIQ delivering research for global consumer brands and leading an 11-person team, so I start from the business problem, not the model.

Publication

First author

SenticLens: A Production Sentiment-Analysis Framework with Behavioral Auditing of LLM Affective Coherence

Simerjit Kaur Nath, Erik Cambria, Haibo Pen · SENTIRE Workshop at IEEE ICDM 2026 (ICDM is ranked CORE A*) · Accepted, to appear in the Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Data Mining Workshops (ICDMW)

Designed a paired benchmark: a 4,000-review corpus across 4 brands, matching real reviews with synthetic reviews of known sentiment. Across 24,000 audited records and 3 open-weight LLMs, models contradicted themselves ~6× as often on real reviews as on clean data (2.32% vs 0.37%).
